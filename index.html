🍺 Beer Pong

Pass-the-phone · 2 players
How to play
  • Drag back on the lower half — pull = power, left/right = aim
  • Release to throw with a real arc into the cups
  • Sink all 10 of your opponent's cups
  • Pass the phone each turn · Re-rack anytime
